# Rozmiso: sales and profit launch plan

Prepared 6 October 2026. Operational findings use the saved 5 October Shopify audit; homepage/navigation were updated 6 October. Admin settings were not re-audited in this review. Public supplier and Shopify guidance were checked today.

## Priority 1 — make orders possible
1. Obtain an SKU-level written supplier quote: exact product/variant, available quantity, packaging, tax invoice, forward and reverse freight, COD fees/settlement, handling, integration/subscription fees, damaged-item process, customer returns and stock synchronization.
2. Buy and inspect a sample before endorsing performance. Film original footage. Test the fabric shaver on appropriate fabrics using the manufacturer's care instructions.
3. Complete payment onboarding with an India-supported provider; verify actual gateway fees plus the Shopify plan's transaction fees. Owner business, bank and identity details are required for onboarding where requested. Configure COD only after the supplier confirms the workflow.
4. Replace the last-audited domestic shipping charge of INR379 and unsupported timing with the real service/offer. At INR799 plus INR379 shipping, the customer pays INR1178. Decide whether shipping is included or separately charged and model that decision.
5. Complete the real business/return address, customer support details and policies. Verify applicable tax and invoicing treatment with an accountant. Supplier policy is not automatically the store's customer policy.
6. Load confirmed inventory and enable purchases only for approved SKUs. Test checkout, payment, order emails, supplier handoff, dispatch, tracking, delivery, COD reconciliation and refund handling. Verify public access without preview/admin cookies. Remove store password only when ready.

## Priority 2 — establish profitable product economics
Current saved planning model, not contracted costs or measured performance:
- Fabric shaver: INR799 sale; approximately INR38 contribution per placed order at INR180 acquisition cost; estimated break-even acquisition cost INR218.
- Cleaning brush: INR649 sale; approximately INR105 loss per placed order at the same acquisition cost; break-even acquisition cost about INR75.
- Travel pouch: INR349 sale; approximately INR230 loss per placed order. Negative even with no advertising under the present standalone assumptions.
- Soap brush: INR299 sale; approximately INR229 loss per placed order. Negative even with no advertising under the present standalone assumptions.

Recommendation: sample and validate one hero product first. Keep the brush for organic/low-cost traffic only if the revised model supports it. Pouch and soap brush need genuine combined-shipment bundle economics, revised pricing or removal from the commercial launch. Do not call any product a proven winner yet.

Replace all assumed costs, RTO/refund rates and tax treatment before setting a paid acquisition ceiling. Include subscription and app costs, cash timing and income-tax treatment as appropriate. A planning target of INR100-120 acquisition cost for the shaver creates more room than the INR180 base case, but is not evidence that this cost is attainable.

## Priority 3 — acquire and measure first customers
- Create 3-5 original short demonstrations from the inspected sample: problem, actual use, visible result, product detail and honest limitation.
- Build a focused product landing page with real images/video, exact included items, care, stock, total price, delivery coverage and real support/returns.
- Start with Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts plus small creator tests where suitable. Audience hypotheses for the shaver: people maintaining garments and upholstery; validate response rather than assuming a fixed demographic.
- Connect the official Facebook & Instagram channel/Meta pixel with appropriate privacy and consent configuration. Verify view, add-to-cart, checkout and purchase events and compare them against Shopify orders; avoid duplicate purchases.
- Prepare a small capped paid test only after launch gates pass and a total budget is approved. Send ads to the selected product page. Test a few distinct creative angles before increasing spend. No ad spend is authorized by this document.
- Use opted-in follow-up and abandoned-checkout recovery where available. Obtain authentic reviews from actual delivered customers; do not fabricate social proof.

## Daily scorecard
Track spend, sessions, product views, add-to-cart, checkouts, placed orders, dispatched orders, delivered/retained orders, cancellations, RTO, refunds, net collected revenue and all costs. CAC here is spend per placed order to match the current model; separately track spend per retained delivered customer.

Use contribution after ads, delivery losses and refunds as the decision metric. Booked sales and platform ROAS alone are insufficient. Pause/repair loss-making tests; scale gradually only after mature delivered-order cohorts show positive contribution and enough cash remains for dispatch, returns and settlements.

## Owner inputs needed to execute external steps
Actual business/return details; selected supplier and sample approval; payment onboarding/KYC and bank details entered directly into the provider; agreed total test-spend limit and cash reserve. Do not send sensitive credentials or bank documents into chat.

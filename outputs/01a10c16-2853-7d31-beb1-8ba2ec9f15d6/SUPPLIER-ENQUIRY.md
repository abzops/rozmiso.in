## Sample inspection

Photograph unopened package, label and everything inside. Compare model, color, accessories and build with the listing. Check device instructions, power/charging requirements and basic operation. For the shaver, test a hidden area of suitable fabric with light pressure. Stop if it snags, overheats or behaves unexpectedly. Record actual results and defects. Ask for the applicable certification/labeling and invoice details. Do not endorse performance before inspecting the sample.

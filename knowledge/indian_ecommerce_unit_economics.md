# 📊 Indian E-Commerce & Dropshipping Unit Economics Calculator

In the Indian dropshipping ecosystem, calculating margins without accounting for **Cash on Delivery (COD)** and **Return to Origin (RTO)** is the #1 reason beginners fail.

---

## 1. The Real Indian Dropshipping P&L Formula

```
Delivered Revenue = Orders * (1 - RTO_Rate) * Selling_Price
Total COGS = Delivered_Units * Supplier_Cost
Forward Courier Cost = Total_Orders * Forward_Shipping_Fee (approx ₹60)
Reverse Courier Cost (RTO Loss) = (Total_Orders * RTO_Rate) * Reverse_Shipping_Fee (approx ₹60)
Ad Spend = Total_Orders * CAC (Cost Per Acquisition)
Payment Gateway Fee = (Prepaid_Orders * 2%) + (COD_Orders * COD_Handling_Fee)

NET PROFIT = Delivered Revenue - (COGS + Forward Courier + Reverse Courier + Ad Spend + Gateway Fees)
```

## 2. Benchmark Unit Economics for a ₹999 Product

Assumptions:
- **Product Sourcing Price**: ₹250 (on Roposo Clout or GlowRoad)
- **Selling Price**: ₹999
- **Markup**: ~4.0x
- **Ad Spend per Purchase (Meta CAC)**: ₹280
- **Total Orders Placed**: 100
- **COD Ratio**: 70% | **Prepaid Ratio**: 30%
- **Managed RTO Rate**: 18% (18 orders returned, 82 delivered)

| Metric | Calculation | Amount |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross Orders Generated** | 100 Orders | - |
| **Delivered Orders (82%)** | 82 Orders × ₹999 | **₹81,918** |
| **Product COGS** | 82 Delivered Units × ₹250 | -₹20,500 |
| **Forward Courier Cost** | 100 Orders × ₹65 | -₹6,500 |
| **Reverse Courier Cost (RTO Fee)** | 18 Returned Orders × ₹65 | -₹1,170 |
| **Meta Ad Spend (CAC)** | 100 Orders × ₹280 | -₹28,000 |
| **COD Collection & PG Fees** | Approx ₹25/order on delivered | -₹2,050 |
| **Packaging & Miscellaneous** | ₹15/order on delivered | -₹1,230 |
| **TOTAL EXPENSES** | | **-₹59,450** |
| **NET PROFIT IN POCKET** | **₹81,918 - ₹59,450** | **₹22,468** |
| **NET PROFIT MARGIN** | **₹22,468 / ₹81,918** | **27.4%** |

## 3. Break-Even ROAS Target

To maintain profitability with an 18% RTO and ₹250 sourcing cost:
- Net revenue per delivered unit = ₹999 - ₹250 (product) - ₹100 (courier + RTO reserve) - ₹25 (PG fee) = **₹624 margin available for advertising and profit**.
- If CAC is ₹280, ROAS = ₹999 / ₹280 = **3.56x**.
- **Minimum Break-Even ROAS**: **~2.2x**.
- **Healthy Scaling ROAS**: **3.0x to 4.0x**.

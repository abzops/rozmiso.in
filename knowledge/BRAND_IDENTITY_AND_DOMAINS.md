## 1. The 4 Criteria for a High-Trust Indian D2C Brand Name

In the Indian dropshipping market, where **70% of orders are COD** and customers are naturally skeptical of delivery scams, your brand name must achieve four things:

1. **Avoid Spammy Tropes**: Never use names like `cheapindiadeals.com`, `superhotdropship.com`, or `bazaardiscounts.com`. These trigger immediate distrust and skyrocket RTO refusals.
2. **The "Boutique D2C" Perception**: Names like *Boat, Noise, Portronics, Wakefit, Koparo, Mokobara, Boldfit* work because they sound like funded, quality-focused Indian D2C brands.
3. **Category Versatility**: Must be broad enough to sell a 2-in-1 Car Vacuum, an Acupressure Foot Massager, or an Inkless Pocket Printer under the same umbrella.
4. **Phonetic Clarity**: Must be easy to spell in 2 syllables over WhatsApp support messages or automated IVR verification calls.

## 4. Step-by-Step GoDaddy Purchase & Setup Checklist

1. **Search & Add to Cart**:
   - Go to [godaddy.com](https://www.godaddy.com).
   - Search for your chosen domain (e.g. `veylons.com` or `kovaralife.com`).
   - Standard 1-year registration cost is typically **₹499 to ₹899 + GST**.
2. **Decline Unnecessary Add-ons**:
   - Decline GoDaddy's website builder (you will connect directly to Shopify).
   - Standard domain privacy protection is already included for free by ICANN rules.
   - Decline professional email at checkout if you prefer to set up Google Workspace or Zoho Mail (Zoho offers free custom domain email for up to 5 users in India).
3. **Connecting to Shopify**:
   - In Shopify Admin $\rightarrow$ **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Domains** $\rightarrow$ **Connect existing domain**.
   - Enter your domain name.
   - Click **Connect automatically** (Shopify has 1-click login integration with GoDaddy that configures the `A Record` (`23.227.38.65`) and `CNAME` (`shops.myshopify.com`) in under 2 minutes).

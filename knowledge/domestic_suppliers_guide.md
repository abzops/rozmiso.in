# 📦 Domestic Indian Dropshipping Suppliers Guide

Why Domestic Suppliers?
- **Delivery Speed**: 3 to 5 days across all Indian pin codes (vs 15-25 days from China). Fast delivery is the single biggest factor in keeping COD RTO under 18%.
- **Zero Customs Friction**: No KYC issues, no delayed import duties, no confiscated inventory.
- **Automated Remittance**: Domestic platforms collect COD cash and deposit it into your bank account on fixed weekly or early remittance schedules.

## 2. Supplier Evaluation Checklist

Before importing a product from any supplier:
1. **Stock Availability**: Verify that the supplier has at least 500+ units in local warehouses.
2. **Dispatch SLA**: Sourcing partner must dispatch within **24 to 48 hours**. Any delay past 48 hours inflates cancellation and RTO.
3. **Packaging Weight**: Keep total volumetric weight under **0.5 kg (500 grams)** to qualify for standard lowest-tier shipping slabs (~₹50-₹70).

# ⚖️ Legal Setup, GST Registration & Payment Gateways for India

Setting up your legal and banking foundation correctly protects you from legal penalties and ensures smooth daily cash remittances from COD orders.

---

## 1. Legal Registration Roadmap

### Step 1: MSME / Udyam Registration (Free)
- **Portal**: [udyamregistration.gov.in](https://udyamregistration.gov.in)
- **Cost**: ₹0 (Do not pay fraudulent private agents).
- **Documents Required**:
  - Personal Aadhaar Card (linked to mobile number for OTP).
  - PAN Card.
- **Time to Issue**: Instant registration number; certificate ready in 24–48 hours.
- **Purpose**: Establishes government recognition of your Sole Proprietorship entity and unlocks business banking.

### Step 2: GST (Goods & Services Tax) Registration
- **Portal**: [gst.gov.in](https://www.gst.gov.in)
- **Is GST Mandatory?**: YES. In India, Section 24 of the CGST Act mandates compulsory registration for persons making any inter-state taxable supply of goods via an e-commerce platform, regardless of turnover.
- **Documents Required**:
  - PAN Card of the Proprietor.
  - Aadhaar Card.
  - Passport-size photograph.
  - Electricity bill or property tax receipt of your residence (which acts as the registered business place).
  - Rent agreement + NOC from owner (if rented).
  - Cancelled cheque or bank statement.
- **HSN Codes commonly used for dropshipping**:
  - `3924`: Household plastic & kitchenware articles.
  - `8509`: Electromechanical domestic appliances.
  - `8708`: Parts & accessories for motor vehicles.

### Step 3: Current Bank Account
- Once your GST registration certificate and Udyam registration are active, open a Current Account with:
  - **HDFC Bank / ICICI Bank / IDFC First Bank** (recommended for e-commerce API integrations).
  - This account receives your weekly/bi-weekly COD remittances and payment gateway payouts.

## 2. Payment Gateway Setup (Prepaid Orders)

To capture 20% to 35% of orders via UPI/Credit/Debit cards:
- **Top Gateways in India**: **Razorpay**, **Cashfree Payments**, **PhonePe PG**, **Paytm Payment Gateway**.
- **Recommended**: **Cashfree** or **Razorpay**. Both offer native 1-click Shopify plugins and UPI QR code popups optimized for mobile checkout.
- **Onboarding Requirements**:
  - GSTIN certificate.
  - Business PAN.
  - Current bank account cheque.
  - Live website with mandatory compliance pages (About Us, Contact Us with registered address & phone, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Shipping & Delivery Policy, Refund & Replacement Policy).

---

## 1. The Naming Psychology Behind Meesho & Miniso

Why do Indian middle-class shoppers love **Meesho**, **Miniso**, and **Zomato**?

1. **The Friendly "-o" Ending**: In Indian linguistics, names ending with a soft open vowel (*-o*) roll off the tongue effortlessly in Hindi, Hinglish, Marathi, Gujarati, Telugu, and Tamil. It feels approachable, informal, and non-intimidating.
2. **Emotional Desi Anchors ("Apna" & "Mera")**: Meesho succeeded because it was derived from *"Meri Shop"* (My Shop). Middle-class buyers want a brand that feels like *theirs*—not a snobbish luxury store.
3. **The "Bachat" (Savings) Value Trigger**: The Indian middle class loves smart utility—buying problem-solving gadgets that save time or money without overpaying.

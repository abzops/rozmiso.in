## 2. The 5-Point Vetting Filter for Winning Products in India

Every product in this catalog has been strictly vetted against the **Hunter-Dot Indian Viability Filter**:

1. **Sourcing Price Threshold**: Base price ≤ ₹350 on domestic wholesale platforms (to support a minimum 3.0x to 4.0x retail markup).
2. **Shipping Weight Slab**: Volumetric & dead weight strictly under **500 grams (0.5 kg)** to stay within the baseline courier freight slab (₹55–₹70 pan-India).
3. **Transit Resilience**: Zero glass, zero delicate ceramic, and zero unshielded hazardous liquids. Must withstand rugged sorting on Indian courier conveyor belts.
4. **Visual "Problem-Solver" or "Aesthetic Wow"**: Must stop the Instagram Reels scroll within 3 seconds without requiring complicated explanations.
5. **High Impulse Perceived Value**: Target retail price between **₹799 and ₹1,499**—the psychological sweet spot for Indian middle-class COD purchases.

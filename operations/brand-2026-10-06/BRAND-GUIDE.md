# Rozmiso brand system

Updated 6 October 2026. Brand spelling: **Rozmiso**.

## Positioning and voice
Practical everyday essentials for home care, kitchen routines and days away.
Core line: **Little upgrades. Everyday possibilities.**
Supporting line: **Simple things. Better days.**
Write clear descriptions of purpose and use. Keep copy calm, specific and helpful. State current product availability accurately. Use evidence for delivery, performance and service claims.

## Identity
The rounded forest tile contains a bold cream R. Use it as the browser favicon, compact brand mark and alongside the Rozmiso wordmark. Keep the mark and wordmark readable; do not stretch, add gradients or put detailed illustrations inside the favicon. The same SVG master generates all supplied PNG sizes.

- Forest: #26342e — wordmark, primary actions and the story panel.
- Cream: #fbfaf6 — main canvas and reversed identity.
- Sage: #e8ece1 — supporting surfaces.
- Clay: #b75132 — small highlights and focus/active states.
- Muted ink: #65706a — supporting text on light surfaces.
- Pale sage: #c3ccc5 — supporting text on forest surfaces.

## Typography and UI
Manrope for wordmark and headings; DM Sans for body text and controls. Consistent 12px card corners, 10px buttons, generous section spacing and simple outline SVG icons. Keep the search prominent and touch controls comfortably sized. Reduce decorative movement when the visitor requests reduced motion.

## Assets
- rozmiso-mark.svg: editable vector master.
- rozmiso-brand-16.png / 32.png: small icon exports.
- rozmiso-brand-180.png: touch icon export.
- rozmiso-brand-512.png: large brand mark export.

The theme contains a favicon image setting under Brand. When left blank, it uses the supplied R icon, with a vector favicon and PNG fallback. The 180px touch icon fallback is embedded in the theme. Existing stock/order and payment conditions are outside this visual update.

## Visible design findings

1. **Gallery consistency:** nine thumbnails include repeated near-identical supplier shots, some promotional labels/text and an inset showing a spray bottle. These distract from the exact physical product and can confuse package contents. Use six distinct verified views, including exact product, rear bristle plane, top fill-cap detail and correct use pose. Product image source must not invent body parts or orient the bristle plane incorrectly.
2. **Information hierarchy:** title, price, availability note, quantity, disabled primary button and enquiry occupy a long stack before benefits. The price is easy to find, but the primary product benefit and package contents are lower. A redesigned summary can combine concise factual copy, price, quantity and availability-driven action with less empty vertical space.
3. **Product education:** the description is a short paragraph plus bullets. There is no visible full-width use-case explanation, clearly separated what-is-included block or dedicated care block before recommendations. Restructure verified facts into readable live text, not only poster text.
4. **Package clarity:** existing copy says one brush and no cleaning liquid. Make this easy to scan and keep props out of included-item assumptions. Do not copy unverified mechanism/dimension/material/capacity claims into generated concepts.
5. **Below-fold space:** gallery ends substantially before the right-side details; then considerable blank space precedes recommendations. Introduce purposeful detail/use/care content and balanced gallery layout rather than enlarge decorative gaps.
6. **Support disclosure:** delivery/payment and returns/support accordions exist but were collapsed during capture. Their presence is visible; their text/operational accuracy was not audited here. Proposed layouts should reserve clear areas without inventing delivery, COD, return-window or warranty facts.

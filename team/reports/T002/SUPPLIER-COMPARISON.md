### Service terms and missing costs

- [Dropshipping FAQ](https://dropshipping.deodap.com/faqs.php): supplier states dispatch 24–48 hours, typical delivery 3–7 business days depending on destination, COD/prepaid, white-label packaging and tracking. It gives `dropshipping@deodap.com` as the published enquiry contact. These statements are not a Rozmiso SLA, nationwide COD eligibility or a fee schedule.
- [Plans](https://dropshipping.deodap.com/pricing.php): Shopify and CSV offerings describe daily stock updates/order management; professional photos are advertised for reselling. No dependable numeric existing-store Shopify/CSV fee was established from this page. Existing Rozmiso does not need a second website merely because a bundled plan advertises one.
- [Dropshipping terms](https://dropshipping.deodap.com/terms-and-conditions.php), sections 5 and 8: service payments are described as non-refundable; defect/damage/wrong/missing-item claims require a clear unboxing video and notification within 48 hours. Return freight falls to the dropshipper and original/return shipping is described as non-refundable. FAQ photo-proof wording is less specific than the video requirement. Resolve the applicable written process and any first-week guarantee before payment.
- **Unknown:** exact outbound rate by packed weight/PIN, COD fee and remittance, RTO charge and stock credit, packing/handling, wallet/deposit rules, stock-sync method, delivery exceptions, plan cancellation and tax-inclusive account price. A public retail free-shipping banner does not establish dropshipping shipping cost.
- **Image rights:** photos are publicly shown and some plans advertise provision. A specific commercial reuse/editing/advertising license for Rozmiso was not evidenced. Obtain written permission for the actual SKU media, including any logos or third-party imagery.

**Feasibility judgment:** Best evidenced route for a written fabric-shaver quote and one approved sample. Pause cleaning-brush procurement/listing promises until restock and variant identity are confirmed. The pouch/soap brush can be evaluated as combined-shipment items only after incremental freight and packing costs are quoted; this audit does not establish that they are profitable add-ons.

## 3. vFulfill — managed RFQ/stock model; capital and fit unknown

[Current platform](https://vfulfill.io/) and [pricing](https://vfulfill.io/pricing/) describe sourcing and Indian fulfillment. No public exact SKU/price/stock match was verified for any of the four products. The product links above are **RFQ inputs**, not vFulfill catalog matches.

- [Operational FAQ](https://knowledgebase.vfulfill.io/en/articles/14977737-how-vfulfill-works-frequently-asked-questions): external product links may be evaluated through RFQ; sample availability is conditional. Its Stockshipping model generally requires 100 units, with product/supplier variation. COD is for eligible shipments; typical delivery is 3–7 days after dispatch. It states wallet COD remittance Monday/Thursday, and RTO generally costs forward freight plus ₹5 handling; customer returns depend on the applicable policy. Confirm whether this stock model or a different one-piece service would apply to Rozmiso.
- [Pricing](https://vfulfill.io/pricing/) advertises Mover at US$79/month and a convenience fee expressed as ₹50 or 5% of delivered order value. Plan-dependent remittance wording differs from the operational FAQ. Confirm billing period/currency/taxes, how the “or” fee is applied, other handling/storage/optional charges and actual settlement schedule before comparing economics.
- [Rate-card guide](https://knowledgebase.vfulfill.io/en/articles/11125370-exploring-the-service-shipping-rate-cards) directs users to rate cards, including weight/COD/RTO considerations. No account-specific destination/weight freight table was accessed or quoted in this audit.
- Product media being advertised is not a verified license for Rozmiso. Exact model, shipment warehouse, India availability, procurement lead time, MOQ, sample price, inspection standard, return rights and image-use permission remain RFQ questions.

**Feasibility judgment:** Keep as a later/backup sourcing comparison if its MOQ, working capital and recurring/per-order charges fit an approved budget. Do not infer zero inventory commitment or immediate domestic dispatch from older generic dropshipping descriptions. No account or paid plan is needed merely to label it a research candidate.

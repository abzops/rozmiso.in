Overview

A wheeled support for carefully repositioning a two-wheeler with a flat tyre. It supports movement and does not repair or inflate the tyre.

Features

  • Galvanized steel frame.
  • Metal tyre cradle with compact wheels.
  • Compact format for storage.

Specifications

  • Type: Manual flat-tyre support dolly
  • Frame material: Galvanized steel
  • Appearance: Silver frame; off-white wheels with pink hubs

Included items

  • One support dolly, as pictured.

How to use

  • Check tyre fit and the supplied instructions before use.
  • Position securely under the tyre and push manually on a suitable level surface.
  • Never ride with the dolly fitted.

Care

  • Wipe with a damp cloth after use.
  • Store dry.

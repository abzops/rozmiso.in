Overview

A compact rechargeable blender for single servings of fruit drinks and shakes. A clear jar sits on a pink motor base, with a carry strap on the lid.

Features

  • 380 ml jar.
  • USB rechargeable design.
  • Lid with carry strap.

Specifications

  • Type: Portable rechargeable personal blender
  • Capacity: 380 ml, supplier listed
  • Appearance: Pink lid and base, clear jar, black control panel

Included items

  • One portable blender with jar, motor base and strapped lid.

How to use

  • Add small pieces of soft fruit and sufficient liquid.
  • Close the lid securely and operate according to the supplied instructions.
  • Use cold or room-temperature ingredients; avoid hot liquids.

Care

  • Switch off before cleaning and keep hands away from blades.
  • Wash detachable cup and lid; keep the motor base and charging port dry.
  • Dry parts before reassembly.

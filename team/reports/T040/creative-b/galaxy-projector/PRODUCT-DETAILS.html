Overview

A standing astronaut projector for decorative star and nebula lighting. The adjustable head changes the projection direction.

Features

  • Standing astronaut design with a moon-style base.
  • Adjustable projection angle.
  • Remote control for lighting settings.

Specifications

  • Type: Decorative tabletop light projector
  • Body material: Plastic
  • Appearance: White astronaut, black visor and textured base

Included items

  • One astronaut projector.
  • Remote control, according to the supplier listing.

How to use

  • Place on a stable surface.
  • Follow the supplied power instructions.
  • Adjust the angle and lighting settings as directed in the manual.

Care

  • Wipe with a soft dry cloth.
  • Keep dry and avoid looking directly into the light.

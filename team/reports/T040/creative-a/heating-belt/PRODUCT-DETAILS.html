Overview

A rechargeable waist belt that combines adjustable warmth with vibration. Its lavender front unit has a black digital display and sits on an adjustable grey strap for use around the waist.

Features

  • Rechargeable design for cordless use.
  • Heating and vibration functions.
  • Adjustable waist strap.
  • Digital display and simple controls.

How to use

  1. Read the supplied instructions before charging or first use.
  2. Charge using the connection and power source specified in those instructions.
  3. Position the belt around the waist and adjust the strap for a comfortable fit.
  4. Use the controls to select the setting described in the instructions. Stop if the belt feels uncomfortable or too warm.
  5. Switch off after use.

Care

Switch off and disconnect before cleaning. Keep the electronic unit and charging connection dry. Follow the supplied cleaning instructions; do not assume that the complete unit can be washed or immersed.

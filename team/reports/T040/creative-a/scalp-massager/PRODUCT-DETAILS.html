Overview

A compact USB rechargeable scalp massager with a rounded white shell, silver trim and four grey massage heads. The handheld design has a single top button for operation.

Features

  • Four detachable massage heads with soft silicone bristles.
  • USB rechargeable for cordless use.
  • Rounded handheld body.
  • One-button control.

How to use

  1. Read the supplied instructions and charge as directed before use.
  2. Hold the unit comfortably and use the top button as instructed.
  3. Place the heads gently against the scalp and move slowly without pressing hard.
  4. Stop if it catches the hair or causes discomfort.
  5. Switch off after use.

Care

Switch off and disconnect before cleaning. The supplier recommends wiping the silicone heads with a damp cloth and avoiding harsh chemicals. Follow the supplied instructions for removing and refitting heads. Keep the body and charging connection dry; do not immerse the unit or assume it is suitable for shower use.

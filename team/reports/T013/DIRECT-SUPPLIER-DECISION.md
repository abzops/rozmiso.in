## What to find out before choosing

Shiva owns the supplier evidence; Codex can prepare the comparison and draft, then send only after a named recipient/channel and contact authority are supplied. No enquiry has been sent in this task.

Ask each shortlisted supplier for one dated answer package:

1. Can an existing Shopify store place one-piece orders to separate customers without bulk inventory, paid website setup, mandatory subscription or deposit? State every recurring/onboarding/per-order fee and tax, minimum wallet top-up, withdrawal and cancellation terms.
2. Identify the exact SKU/model/color/accessories, available quantity, stock feed and shortage process; quote a sample before any commitment.
3. Quote packed weight/size, packing, outbound/COD/RTO/return charges and serviceability for actual target PINs. State whether fees are per item or parcel and whether multi-item orders share freight.
4. Explain when supplier payment is due, COD settlement/withdrawal, rejected delivery, RTO product-value credit, lost shipment, cancellation and refund cash timing.
5. Provide the applicable merchant agreement and specific dispatch/defect/return procedure, authorized return destination and evidence deadlines. Resolve conflicts between FAQs, policy and plan pages.
6. Provide original exact-model multi-angle images, dimensions, included-items photo, genuine demonstration video and instructions. Explicitly authorize Shopify/organic/paid-ad reuse, crop/edit and use as reference for faithful AI presentation; identify third-party restrictions and watermark terms.
7. For Baapstore specifically: confirm the no-subscription order workflow and supply the real current free-tier catalog, reconciling 60 advertised products versus one extracted product and the subscription-required guides.

Acceptance evidence before sales: written one-piece terms with no unapproved commitment; physically inspected sample; confirmed exact variant/stock; complete landed/delivered/RTO/refund costs; eligible licensed media; an end-to-end trial order and settlement record. Only then substitute quotes into Finance scenarios. No fixed markup, public discount, generalized supplier margin or AI image establishes customer trust or profit.

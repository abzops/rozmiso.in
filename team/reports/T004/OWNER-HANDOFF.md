## 4. Approve supplier terms and inspect a sample

**Where:** The chosen supplier's verified communication or ordering channel. The prepared [supplier enquiry](../../../outputs/01a10c16-2853-7d31-beb1-8ba2ec9f15d6/SUPPLIER-ENQUIRY.md) is a draft, not a sent message or agreement.

**Why:** Public product listings do not confirm a dropshipping relationship, real available stock, image permissions, landed cost or returns support.

**Your decisions/actions:**

1. Review the team's supplier comparison. Choose a candidate and either send the prepared enquiry directly or explicitly authorize Codex to contact that named supplier through the agreed channel. Codex can handle preparation and follow-up where tools and authorization permit.
2. Review the written quote and fulfillment terms. Confirm the exact SKU/variant, stock, total sample/order costs, taxes/invoicing, packaging, delivery coverage, dispatch expectations, payment/COD settlement if offered, failed-delivery/RTO charges, customer returns, approved return destination, warranty/replacement handling and permission to use product images.
3. Approve any agreement or paid plan yourself. Purchase the sample only within the approved limit and after its total price is known.
4. Physically inspect it: correct item and contents, condition, packaging/labels, actual size, instructions and safe use, performance, and defects. Record your own photos or short demonstration if you permit the brand to use them. Agents cannot inspect an item they have never handled.

**Return:** A quote/terms summary or redacted supplier document, exact product identifiers, permission/stock evidence and your sample checklist result: pass, issues or reject. Remove private account, identity, banking or customer details before sharing. A supplier's public stock indication is not permission to invent a Shopify inventory number.

**Codex does next:** Recalculate margins, recommend whether the product is worth testing, prepare stock/shipping settings from confirmed evidence, and draft sample-backed product content. No product is a proven winner at this stage.

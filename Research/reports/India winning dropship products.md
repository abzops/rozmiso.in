# Heat, relief and gifting win Rozmiso's winter

The five strongest DeoDap products for Rozmiso to test on Meta and Instagram from November 2026 to January 2027 come from five different categories. **(1) a rechargeable menstrual cramp heating belt** (women's wellness), **(2) a 4-head electric scalp massager** (personal care and gifting), **(3) a 500 W portable room heater** (winter home appliance, only if BIS certification checks out), **(4) an astronaut galaxy projector** (decor and Christmas/New Year gifting) and **(5) a portable USB juicer** (kitchen). The backups are a motorcycle flat-tyre booster, an electric lint remover, an electric nail clipper and the mini electric garlic chopper. Each top-5 pick sells for Rs 749–1,299, which keeps it inside DeoDap's listed MRP and roughly 2.5–3x its listed DeoDap price. At 25% COD RTO, that leaves **about Rs 250–450 contribution per shipped order and a breakeven ROAS of about 2.7–3.1x** on Meta-reported revenue. That is achievable, but only with hard RTO controls. The demand evidence is real but indirect. It rests on DeoDap's own curated "customer-favorites" and "fan-favorites" collections, global ad-spy reports and Indian seasonal press data. **The Meta Ad Library (India), Amazon.in Movers & Shakers, Instagram and YouTube could not be read live** (HTTP 403/503 or login walls). So no pick has India ad-longevity proof yet, and a one-hour manual Ad Library check should come before any spend. These picks also move Rozmiso up from its current Rs 149–799 home, kitchen and travel range into the Rs 749–1,299 band. Partial COD and order confirmation therefore matter more than they do today.

## A Rs 999 order clears about Rs 300–370 after logistics and RTO

All margins here come from one transparent model, built from the cost benchmarks in the notes. Forward shipping is **Rs 80** on every shipped order. The COD fee is **Rs 40** on delivered orders, in line with the Rs 25–50 flat-fee range ([ShipPrime](https://shipprime.live/resources/blogs/how-to-calculate-shipping-costs-india)) and Shiprocket's illustrative Rs 36 ([Shiprocket](https://support.shiprocket.in/support/solutions/articles/152000000168-how-to-check-the-applied-cod-charges-)). Return freight is **Rs 80** on each RTO. The base RTO rate is **25%**, the middle of the 20–30% national COD range ([base.com](https://base.com/en-IN/blog/?p=22444); [Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/cash-on-delivery-and-the-challenges/)). The model also assumes DeoDap restocks RTO units at no product cost to Rozmiso. That assumption is unverified, and if it is wrong every breakeven below gets worse. It also ignores GST, payment-gateway fees and packaging.

On those terms, contribution per shipped order is 0.75 × (price − cost) − Rs 130. Breakeven ROAS on Meta-reported revenue is the price divided by that contribution. Agency data shows why this matters: reported ROAS of 2.5x can net out to about **1.6x after COD RTO** ([upgrowth](https://upgrowth.in/how-to-improve-roas-d2c-india-2026/)). Orders of **Rs 500–1,000 carry the highest RTO, about 28%** ([EasySell](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/cod-order-value-rto-rate-price-range-delivery)), and tier-3+ cities can run 25–35% ([Socioez](https://socioez.com/meta-ads-india-ecommerce-playbook/)). The table therefore also shows a 35% RTO case.

The contribution figure is also the **breakeven cost per Meta purchase**. The heating belt can afford about Rs 370 per COD order, the juicer only about Rs 250. Indian Meta benchmarks put CPM at roughly **Rs 50–200** and Reels CPC at **Rs 8–18**, with e-commerce CTR around 1.3% ([OwlClaw](https://owlclaw.com/benchmarks/meta-ads-benchmarks-india)). Budget for **20–30% CPM inflation** in festive and peak periods ([Exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/festive-season-news/festive-rush-d2c-brands-double-spends-on-regional-markets-cpms-up-30-across-platforms-148281.html)). Benchmark CPM figures conflict widely, so treat the first Rs 500–1,000 a day of tests per product as the real benchmark ([Bcom2ecom](https://bcom2ecom.in/blog/dropshipping-in-india-guide)).

**Pricing rule.** Set price so that breakeven ROAS at 25% RTO is **at or below 3.0x**. In practice that means the price is at least 2.5x the DeoDap cost for items costing over Rs 350, and at least 3.5x for items under Rs 200. The price must never exceed the printed MRP. Any DeoDap item costing under about Rs 200 needs a bundle, for example two lint removers or a lint remover with a manual roller, to clear Rs 600+. The low-ticket best-sellers (zen bell Rs 118, oil sprayer Rs 101, bag sealer Rs 98, facial ice bowl Rs 105) belong in order bumps that raise AOV at almost no extra shipping cost ([DeoDap best-selling-products](https://deodap.in/collections/best-selling-products/products.json?limit=250)). The model also shows why the old Rs 149–799 range cannot carry paid acquisition. A Rs 175 item sold at Rs 599 already needs **3.2x** to break even. A 2x markup on a Rs 349 item at Rs 699 needs **5.3x**, which is effectively unviable.

**RTO controls.** COD RTO runs **25–30% against 2–3% for prepaid** ([Razorpay](https://razorpay.com/blog/cash-on-delivery-and-the-challenges/)), and COD is 58–64% of tier-2/3 orders but 76–83% of RTO volume ([base.com](https://base.com/en-IN/blog/?p=22444)). Every percentage point of RTO is worth defending. Indian 2026 guides converge on four levers ([Unicommerce](https://unicommerce.com/blog/dropshipping-products-to-sell-in-india/)):

- **Partial COD.** Take an advance of Rs 49–99 on orders above Rs 699.
- **Prepaid discount.** Offer Rs 50–100 off for paying upfront.
- **Order confirmation.** Confirm every COD order by WhatsApp or IVR before handing it to DeoDap.
- **Pincode blocking.** Block pincodes with a history of high RTO.

Two further steps help. Ad creative should show the real product at real scale, because expectation mismatch drives refusals of cheap electronics. Heater ads should run only in North Indian geographies, where cold weather and demand line up.

## Avoid sized apparel, above-MRP pricing and saturated gadgets

The clearest exclusions come from category RTO data. Ethnic wear runs **30–45% RTO** and fashion **25–40%**, against **5–15%** for beauty and personal care and **5–12%** for home care ([base.com](https://base.com/en-IN/blog/?p=22444)). That rules out the woolen kurtis, caps and muffler sets that dominate Meesho's winter listings, despite their demand ([Shiprocket](https://www.shiprocket.in/blog/most-selling-products-on-meesho/)). Beyond apparel, there are four groups to skip. The first is products where a profitable price breaks MRP: the 400 W wall heater (MRP Rs 599) and the massage gun (Rs 556 cost against a Rs 699 MRP). The second is items flagged as saturated globally: vegetable choppers, magnetic car mounts, resistance bands and LED strips ([Copyfy](https://www.copyfy.io/en/blog/best-dropshipping-products-q4)). The third is fragile glass kettles. The fourth is anything whose DeoDap cost pushes the price past Rs 1,299, such as the Rs 1,491 heated lunchbox or the Rs 999 bonfire humidifier. Pet products also fail the economics at DeoDap's MRPs. The pet shower sprayer can only sell at its Rs 699 MRP against a Rs 339 cost, which implies a **5.0x breakeven**. Use pet products only as bundles. Western Q4 staples such as advent calendars and ornaments do not translate to India. Christmas decor that does sell, like DeoDap's Rs 150 Christmas bath mat set, is too cheap to stand alone.

Electronics RTO estimates disagree sharply: one source puts it at **8–18%** and another up to **40%** ([Edesy](https://edesy.in/tools/cod-rto-calculator)). Four of the five top picks are rechargeable or plug-in devices. Order 2–3 units of each and test them for charge, heat and noise before spending on ads. One bad batch can erase a month's margin.

## What still needs manual verification before spend

Several checks could not be done in this research and decide whether the plan works.

**Meta Ad Library, India.** The fetch returned HTTP 403, so there is no India-specific ad-longevity or creative-count data for any pick ([Meta Ad Library](https://www.facebook.com/ads/library/)). Search each product name and Hindi/Hinglish variants with country set to India and "all ads" selected. Look for advertisers running **30–45+ days with 10–50 active creatives**, the range Copyfy treats as the profitable sweet spot ([Copyfy](https://www.copyfy.io/en/blog/best-dropshipping-products-q4)). Very many long-running advertisers points to saturation. None at all points to either an untested product or one that does not work.

**Marketplace checks.** Amazon.in Movers & Shakers (HTTP 503), Flipkart, Meesho trending, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts were also unreadable. Search each pick on Amazon.in and Meesho to see the street price. If Meesho sells the scalp massager at Rs 499, a Rs 999 Shopify price needs a gifting or bundle frame to hold.

**DeoDap buy price.** The DeoDap figures used here are the public site's `price` field. The notes could not confirm whether the dropship or reseller panel price is the same, or whether it includes GST, DeoDap's shipping to the end customer, or COD handling. If DeoDap's dropship price adds Rs 60–100 of shipping, every contribution above falls by roughly that amount. Confirm too whether RTO units cost Rozmiso the product price.

**MRP compliance.** Under Legal Metrology rules, the selling price cannot exceed the printed MRP. DeoDap's compare-at "MRP" in its JSON may not match what is printed on the box. Check the physical label on sample units before setting Rs 999–1,299 prices.

**BIS for heaters.** Room heaters and plug-in electrical appliances may fall under BIS Quality Control Orders. The notes did not research this. Ask DeoDap for the BIS licence number (IS 302 series) for the exact heater SKU and check it on the BIS portal. If there is no valid marking, drop the heater and promote the lint remover.

**Search trends.** Google Trends India curves for each item could not be pulled. A five-minute check of the past five years for "room heater", "lint remover" and "heating belt" will confirm timing.

## Conclusion

The data points to a shift for Rozmiso rather than a list of gadgets. The current Rs 149–799 catalogue cannot pay for paid acquisition under Indian COD economics. The products that can are **Rs 999-class, demo-led, size-free items that solve a felt winter or gifting problem**, and DeoDap's own favourites collections already concentrate them. The practical sequence follows the calendar. Test the heating belt, scalp massager and heater (if it clears BIS) in the first two weeks of November. Lean into gifting creatives through the wedding dates and Christmas. Hand the budget to the projector in late December and the juicer in January.

The biggest unknown is not demand but margin plumbing: what DeoDap actually charges a dropshipper, and whether RTO units come back at no product cost. Those two answers move breakeven ROAS more than any creative will. The first conversation should be with DeoDap's reseller desk, and the second with the Meta Ad Library.

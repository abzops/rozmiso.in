# Rozmiso — Brand Identity & Guidelines

**Version:** 1.0 · **Prepared:** 31 October 2026  
**Purpose:** A shared reference for designers, writers, developers and social-media creators.

This guide consolidates the existing Rozmiso identity, website theme and the owner's creative requirements. It does not introduce a new logo or rename the brand. Practical production recommendations are identified below; they are not claims that every page or social account has already been updated.

---

## 1. The brand

| Item | Guideline |
|---|---|
| Brand name | **Rozmiso**. Use this spelling and capitalization in ordinary text. |
| Customer-facing domain | **rozmiso.in**. This is the owner's chosen brand domain; connection and availability must be verified before publishing a customer link. |
| Instagram | **@rozmiso.in** · [Instagram profile](https://www.instagram.com/rozmiso.in/) |
| Facebook | **Rozmiso** · [Recorded Page](https://www.facebook.com/profile.php?id=61595107991994). Verify the destination before publishing. |
| Core description | “Practical finds for home care, kitchen routines and travel.” |
| Main categories | Home care, Kitchen, Travel, Personal care and Other essentials. |
| Intended impression | Thoughtful, useful, calm, approachable and carefully presented. |

**Positioning:** Rozmiso presents practical everyday products through a consistent, premium-looking shopping experience. The emphasis is on useful details and clear demonstrations, with approachable language and realistic product information.

“Premium” describes the quality of presentation. It must not become an unsupported claim about a product's materials, origin, certification or performance.

### Existing brand language

These lines appear in the current website source and establish the writing direction:

- **Thoughtful finds for a brighter everyday.** — main landing headline.
- **Little details. A better everyday.** — supporting brand line.
- **Make everyday spaces feel better.** — home-care message.
- **The Everyday Edit.** — collection/editorial label.

Use one relevant headline per layout. Do not repeat every brand line on every creative.

## 2. Brand personality and voice

**Calm · Clear · Helpful · Thoughtful · Factual**

Write as a knowledgeable shop assistant who explains a product simply. Use short sentences, familiar words and specific visible benefits. A warm, conversational tone is appropriate; pressure, exaggerated urgency and technical jargon are not.

| Context | Writing approach | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Brand introduction | Warm and concise | Practical finds for home care, kitchen routines and travel. |
| Product description | Explain the item and its use | A compact brush for everyday sink and surface cleaning. |
| Feature panel | One verified fact per point | Top fill cap. Rear bristle bed. Compact grip. |
| Care guidance | Direct and useful | Test a discreet area first. Rinse the bristles after use. |
| Call to action | Describe the next step | View product · Explore home care · Shop all products |
| Customer support | Helpful, specific and courteous | Tell us which product you need help with. |

The examples above illustrate the voice; product-specific statements belong only to the matching product after verification.

### Copy rules

- Use publishable product names. Remove supplier codes from customer-facing titles; retain SKU data internally.
- Distinguish **features**, **included items**, **use instructions** and **care**.
- State dimensions, capacity, power, materials and pack quantity only when supported by reliable product information.
- Never invent reviews, ratings, customer counts, discounts, countdowns, stock, certifications or delivery promises.
- Avoid “miracle,” “guaranteed results,” “best in India,” fake scarcity and unsupported before/after claims.
- Do not describe final product campaigns as “collection previews” or say orders are closed without a current factual reason.
- Avoid generic AI-style slogans and unnecessary exclamation marks. Do not add “AI-generated poster” as advertising copy. Follow applicable platform labeling requirements rather than making false authenticity claims.

## 3. Visual theme

### Everyday Edit: warm ivory and forest green

The shared visual direction combines warm neutral backgrounds, deep green typography, restrained serif headlines and clear product photography. Natural light, soft shadows, quiet botanical accents and uncluttered everyday settings support the theme.

**Selected product-page direction:** **Modern Bento**, concept 05. Its defining structure is a product gallery beside a purchase summary, followed by rounded information cards for details, use, care and support.

This is the selected design direction. It is not a statement that the complete Modern Bento rebuild has passed visual acceptance or is the currently published layout.

### Keep consistent across every product

- Same colour family and headline/body font roles.
- Same logo treatment, information hierarchy, icon style and corner-radius family.
- Same care in lighting, image quality and factual wording.
- Similar cover, feature, use-case and specification layouts across a product series.

Product colours remain accurate. Do not recolour the physical product to make it match the brand palette.

## 5. Typography

| Role | Typeface | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Logo lettering | **Manrope 750, converted to vector outlines** | Use supplied artwork. Do not recreate the wordmark by typing the name. |
| Website/editorial headings | **Georgia**, with Times New Roman/serif fallback | Regular weight; restrained, readable serif headlines. |
| Body, navigation, labels and controls | **DM Sans** | Clear sans-serif text with comfortable spacing. |
| Eyebrow/category label | **DM Sans** | Small uppercase label with moderate tracking. |

The logo font and website headline font serve different roles. Do not replace Georgia headings with Manrope merely because the wordmark uses Manrope.

### Recommended production scale

These are starting points for new layouts, not a mandate to resize every existing page:

- Website body: **14–16 px**, approximately **1.5–1.6** line height.
- Product title: approximately **28 px on phones**, **30–34 px on larger screens**; test long names.
- Information-card heading: approximately **20–26 px**.
- Form controls and primary actions: generally **44–48 px** high, with readable labels.
- Social artwork: size text for a phone-screen preview, not just the full-resolution canvas. Avoid tiny specification paragraphs.

Keep headings and product photos proportionate. Avoid the oversized typography, oversized image blocks and excessive outer margins previously rejected by the owner.

### Logo usage

- Existing kit guidance: keep clear space of at least **one quarter of the R mark's height** on every side.
- Existing kit recommended minimums: **150 px** wide for the complete horizontal lockup, **120 px** for wordmark-only. Current compact website headers use smaller placements; inspect legibility at the actual size rather than treating those placements as a new universal minimum.
- Use the R mark for small placements; keep it centred and comfortably inside a circular profile-image crop.
- Preserve aspect ratio. Use SVG for scalable website/print artwork and transparent PNG where SVG is unsupported.
- Never stretch, squash, skew, rotate, redraw, add a shadow, apply a gradient or place the logo over a busy product detail.
- Do not add a tiny subtitle under the logo or substitute a different R symbol.
- Black/white variants are for limited-colour situations, not a separate everyday theme.

## 7. Product photography and image accuracy

The owner requires professional, consistent product imagery. For newly generated product visuals, use the original product photographs as the authority; AI-generated output must preserve the real item.

### Required treatment

- Soft, natural-looking light; clean highlights and believable shadows.
- Warm ivory or a restrained real-life setting suited to the product.
- Clear product outline, accurate scale relationships and uncluttered props.
- Consistent editing and white balance across a product's image set.
- At least **six useful images per product**: cover/studio, alternate view, feature detail, use case, verified specifications or included items, and care/storage/context.

These six roles are a production framework; use a factual alternative when a role cannot be supported. A specification graphic must not invent measurements.

### Product identity is fixed

Do not change shape, proportions, control placement, colour variant, materials, attachments, pack quantity or operating mechanism. Never substitute a visually similar product and call it identical.

For the pink bear soap brush specifically: preserve the **top fill cap between the ears**, the shallow bear-face shell and the **flat rear bristle bed parallel to the face**. In use, the face points up and the rear bristles touch the cleaning surface. No invented side cap or bristles growing from the bear's feet.

Clearly distinguish props from included accessories. Do not generate or reuse demonstrations that misrepresent the actual product.

## 8. Social-media creative system

Use one recognisable Everyday Edit theme across Instagram and Facebook. Maintain the same logo position, colour roles, serif/sans hierarchy, information-card style and closing action throughout a product series.

### Recommended six-slide carousel

1. **Cover:** accurate product, publishable name and one clear benefit.
2. **Useful detail:** one verified feature with an explanatory close-up.
3. **Use case:** a realistic task with the correct product operation.
4. **Specifications / included items:** only supported facts.
5. **Care / practical guidance:** concise and relevant.
6. **Next step:** product name, `rozmiso.in` and the correct product-link destination.

**Recommended formats:** square **1080 × 1080** for a consistent carousel; **1080 × 1920** for a new vertical video/story. Existing square artwork may be larger. Preserve the complete approved composition when adapting it; do not stretch or trim important content to fit.

Keep critical text away from edges and platform controls. Preview every slide on a phone. Avoid dense paragraphs, mismatched fonts, random stickers, excessive badges and fake review stars.

The owner's distribution preference is to share product posts to both Instagram and the connected Facebook Page. Verify the selected destinations at publication time. Instagram captions do not provide a clickable product URL in the same way as a website button; ensure the actual available profile, story or shop-link route works.

## 9. Advertisement videos

- Identify the exact visible product model before accepting a sourced clip.
- Show a useful task and the correct mechanism; do not use a generic lookalike demonstration.
- Review resolution, framing, text legibility, supplier branding and any embedded price or delivery claims.
- A publicly viewable video is not automatically free for commercial reuse. Keep the source, download permission and reuse evidence with the file.
- Keep supplier watermarks and logos intact unless the relevant permission allows modification. Do not present another company's branded ad as a finished Rozmiso creative.
- Check audio/music rights separately from visual reuse permission.
- Keep uncertain matches and reference-only clips separate from publishable assets.

## 10. Website and interface application

- Preserve the warm ivory/forest system from landing page through product page, search and cart.
- Use restrained line icons, rounded cards and subtle borders. Avoid decorative clutter and heavy effects.
- Keep product names, prices, options, quantities and availability tied to real Shopify data.
- Keep the selected thumbnail visibly marked and its image description synchronized.
- Support main-image swiping on touch devices while preserving page scrolling, pinch gestures and tap-to-zoom.
- Use explicit image-frame choices: no accidental pale side bars; do not crop away product parts to fill a frame.
- Check phone, tablet and desktop layouts at their real sizes. A valid build or automated test does not establish visual acceptance.

- [ ] Rozmiso spelling, logo variant and domain are correct.
- [ ] Colours and font roles follow this guide.
- [ ] Product model, variant, mechanism and included quantity match the original references.
- [ ] Product information and promotional claims are supported.

- [ ] Download/reuse permissions and soundtrack rights are recorded where relevant.

- [ ] Platform labeling and accessibility requirements are followed; image descriptions are accurate.
- [ ] Both intended social destinations are checked before cross-posting.

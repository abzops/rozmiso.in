**Hook in 2 seconds**Open on the problem or the wow moment. No logo, no intro.

**Burn in captions**Most people watch Reels muted, so every line of voiceover also goes on screen.

**Keep the bottom 35% clear**Instagram's buttons and caption cover it. Put text in the upper-middle.

**Real hands, real room**Phone camera, daylight, an Indian home. Phone-shot ads usually beat polished studio footage.

How to run them

## Test plan

**Structure.** One campaign per product, Sales objective, Advantage+ audience. Run 3 ads per product: Reel A, Reel B and Photo 1. Spend ₹500–700 a day per product for 3 days before you judge anything.

**Kill rules.** Pause an ad if the cost per link click is above ₹25, or if ₹600 is spent with no add-to-cart. Pause a product if ₹1,500 is spent with no purchase.

**Scale rule.** If ROAS (sales revenue per ₹1 of ad spend) stays above 3.0 for 3 days, raise the budget 20–30% a day. Copy the winning Reel with 3 new hooks instead of making new videos from scratch.

**Cutting returns.** Give ₹50 off for prepaid orders, confirm every COD order on WhatsApp before you ship, and block pincodes that keep refusing deliveries.

**Calendar.** Belt, massager and booster start in Week 1 of Nov. Push gifting creatives across 21 Nov – 13 Dec (wedding dates) and Christmas. The projector starts 1 Dec and the juicer 26 Dec.

**Before spending.** Order 1 unit of each from DeoDap and shoot your own Reels. Check the product works as the listing says. Ask DeoDap whether you may use their photos in ads.

# Rozmiso Reels: brand-voice scripts (v3)

Written with /ig-reel against `ROZMISO-BRAND-GUIDELINES.md`:
- **Voice:** calm, clear, helpful, factual.
- **Every Reel follows these rules:** no fake urgency, no invented results, no exclamation marks, and only facts taken from the DeoDap listings.

Hooks were scored and scripts timed with the ig-reel tools. Python isn't installed on this PC, so they ran through a PowerShell port that uses the same rules and weights. Scripts are timed at 150 words per minute against a 30-second target.

The spoken language is Hinglish (Hindi with English product words) for Indian buyers. The voiceover is generated from these lines. On-screen text is a separate track: 6 words or fewer per card, sentence case, inside the safe zone (y 230–1440, right 230 px clear).

## Visual treatment (Everyday Edit, from the brand guide)

| Element | Treatment |
|---|---|
| Hook card (0–2.5 s) | Ivory `#F5F0E7` rounded card, forest `#173E33` **Georgia** headline, top third (y ≈ 260) |
| Body captions | **DM Sans** semibold, white with a soft dark shadow, centred at y ≈ 1250 (inside the safe zone) |
| Price card (last 6 s) | Ivory card: Georgia price in forest, DM Sans "Cash on Delivery" on a sage `#E7ECDF` strip |
| End frame | Rozmiso **primary logo** (supplied PNG/SVG, unchanged) on ivory, then "Tap Shop Now" |
| Colour grade | Natural and warm. No heavy vignette, no saturated yellow, no red strike-through, no blinking text |
| Sound | Voiceover, soft whoosh on cuts, one gentle hit on the price card. No music; add licensed or in-app audio when posting |

## The current v2 Reels break these brand rules (fix before publishing)
- Yellow boxes, Arial, all-caps urgency and exclamation marks are off-palette and off-voice.
- "Papa ne wapas hi nahi diya" reads as an invented customer story.
- "18 December se pehle order karo" is a delivery promise that hasn't been checked.
- "Kuch hi seconds mein garmi" and "10 minute salon jaisa" are performance claims that haven't been checked.
- The "MRP" strike-throughs show a discount. Use them only if DeoDap's printed MRP is confirmed on the box.

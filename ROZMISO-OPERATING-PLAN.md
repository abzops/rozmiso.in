### Finance is a shared control process

Abhinand approves prices, spending and financial execution. Shiva supplies supplier invoices/charges, outbound and return costs, COD/courier settlement evidence and operational exceptions. Roshan supplies campaign spend, enquiries, sales records, refund cases and customer outcomes. Finance agents consolidate and calculate; Abhinand reviews the result. A refund case recorded by Roshan is not a refund issued. If routine refund authority is later delegated, document its limits and conditions first; applicable obligations must still be met.

**Codex is the single AI orchestrator and point of contact.** All three humans can bring their workstream facts or blockers here. Codex creates bounded tasks, dispatches workers, collects evidence, obtains independent review, integrates accepted work and updates the shared task board. Director helps with briefs and priorities; it is not a second orchestrator.

There is one accepted Antigravity CLI connection for bounded supplied-text tasks. Full native tool execution and simultaneous separation of two accounts are not established. Codex handles dispatch and reports; the humans do not need to copy worker prompts. A/B remain role lanes until actual authenticated isolation is proved. No always-on monitor, automated order bot, courier connector or marketing daemon is implied.

Only Codex writes `team/TASK-BOARD.json`. Human/worker evidence goes in the assigned task reports. One writer per file scope; authors do not review their own work. Department recommendations are submitted evidence, not permission to launch or spend. Existing authorization is reused rather than repeatedly requested.

## Order, support and money handoffs

Before launch, agree where private customer records live in the actual business system and grant only necessary authorized access. Do not copy names, phone numbers, addresses, identity documents or payment credentials into portable AI prompts/reports. Shared reports can use order/case IDs and redacted evidence.

1. **Roshan → Shiva:** An enquiry that becomes an actual valid order moves with order ID, verified payment/COD state, SKU/quantity, serviceability and needed exceptions through the authorized system. An enquiry form is not an order. Do not hand off an unpaid or unconfirmed order as dispatch-ready.
2. **Shiva → Roshan:** Confirmed supplier acceptance, dispatch/tracking and delivery updates; then Roshan communicates accurate updates through the monitored channel. Missing stock or tracking becomes an exception, not an invented delivery date.
3. **Roshan → Shiva → Abhinand:** A return/refund case includes reason and case/order ID; Shiva checks shipment/item/supplier evidence; Abhinand approves financial handling under policy/defined authority. Roshan records the resolution and communication. Urgent cases are escalated promptly, not held for the weekly meeting.
4. **Shiva + Roshan → Finance → Abhinand:** Match costs, sales, returns, fees and settlements; distinguish money expected from money actually received. Abhinand reviews discrepancies and next spending decision.
5. **All humans → Codex:** Bring the task ID, outcome, evidence location, blocker and next decision. Codex produces the next bounded assignment and maintains one accepted view of progress.

## Phases and gates

**Phase 1 — build and prove readiness.** Continue approved theme deployment alongside supplier, seller/support and budget work. Draft support and content can progress without sales, but product claims and operating promises wait for evidence.

**Sales gate:** Confirm real product/stock and service areas, sample pass, supplier and returns arrangements, reviewed economics/cash requirements, applicable seller/payment/tax setup, accurate customer policies, monitored support and tested order/fulfillment flow. Abhinand decides launch after Codex's readiness review. Design deployment, a public storefront or a provider login alone does not meet this gate.

**Phase 2 — one controlled product test.** Choose the candidate with the strongest verified evidence, rather than promising four winning products. Prefer a single trackable offer and channel. Agree test volume, spend ceiling and stop conditions before publishing or buying traffic; a zero-ad-spend organic test is possible if channels and product readiness permit. Check dispatch and support alongside enquiries/conversion.

**Phase 3 — refine after outcomes settle.** Evaluate paid acquisition against retained/settled order contribution, defects, RTO/refunds and cash timing. Pending sales do not prove profit. Revise product, shipping, page or creative from evidence. Do not scale just because gross ROAS looks attractive.

**Phase 4 — expand and automate selectively.** Add products or a backup supplier only after repeatable fulfillment and service. Automate one demonstrated bottleneck at a time: inventory updates, order routing, tracking or reporting. Confirm actual connectors, permissions, charges, failure handling and human override before reliance. No new warehouse, hire, subscription or always-on agent is assumed.

## Proposed daily and weekly rhythm

Agree real availability before adopting these routines. They describe check-ins during active work, not a promised unattended schedule.

**Each working day:**

- Abhinand reviews Codex's priority/result summary and resolves access, budget, creative or launch decisions that block work.
- Shiva checks stock/dispatch exceptions, delayed/NDR/return cases and supplier charges; flags risks affecting any proposed offer.
- Roshan checks the support queue at the agreed coverage times, records enquiries/sales/refund/campaign events and escalates urgent cases. Every open case has an owner and next action.
- Each person reports three facts: completed with evidence, blocked on whom/what, and next useful action. Codex updates the board during the active session. No human waits for all functions to finish before handing in independent evidence.

**Weekly review:**

1. Finance reconciles cost/payment/settlement/refund records from Shiva and Roshan; Abhinand reviews actual cash and contribution versus assumptions.
2. Shiva reviews delivery, defects and return causes; Roshan reviews customer questions, channel results and support gaps; Abhinand reviews brand/store issues.
3. Codex recommends the next small set of tasks with owners and acceptance evidence. Decide continue, revise or stop the offer/test from actual results and cash exposure.
4. Review Roshan's starting assignment and workload, backups/coverage and tool permissions. Only then consider extra roles, spend or automation.

Useful measures are actual sessions/enquiries/orders, conversion when reliably measured, acquisition cost, dispatch/delivery outcomes, RTO/refunds with clear denominators, settled contribution, cash awaiting settlement and unresolved support cases. Until measured, mark these **unknown**, not target achieved.
